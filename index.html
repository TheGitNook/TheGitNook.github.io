A Number-Theoretic Refinement of Stability Conditions
for Queues with Periodic Arrivals: A Shell Filter Approach

Proposes a refined stability condition λpeak < μ(p−1)/p for queueing systems with prime-periodic arrival structures, derived from the L1 shell mod-p filter limit theorem. Applications to emergency medicine, tourism, and urban transit demonstrate that the refined condition correctly identifies structural instability where the classical condition ρ < 1 fails.
